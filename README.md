## 💡 Key Insights
- **Monthly contracts churn the most (34.69%)**, more than double annual contracts (14.36%).
- **Cash payers churn the most (34.12%)**, compared with ~24–27% for digital payment methods.
- **Customers with more support tickets churn more:** churn rises from ~21% at 0 tickets to ~44–50% at 5–9 tickets.

## 💼 Recommendation
Encourage monthly customers to move to longer contracts, and follow up early with customers who raise multiple support tickets.
